# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about safety and quality concerns.
- Welcome questions and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, repository policies, and responsible disclosure boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted intimidation.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Knowingly encouraging unsafe use, credential exposure, policy bypass, or deceptive validation.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when someone is officially representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and applied consistently.

To report a conduct concern privately, contact the maintainer through a private method listed on [Joonas Tanner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/joonastanner). If no private method is available, open a neutral issue requesting private contact without including sensitive incident details. Immediate threats should also be reported through the relevant platform safety channel.
